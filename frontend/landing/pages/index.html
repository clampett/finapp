A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A clearer starting point.

Built for exploration, with the assumptions in view.

Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. FinApp uses manually entered scenarios, demo profiles, and FinApp JSON imports. It does not connect to your bank or execute transactions.

Where are my scenario edits saved?

Scenario edits are saved in this browser on this device, separately for each profile. Export a JSON copy to back up your work or move it to another device. Creating an account does not yet sync scenario edits between devices.

Are forecasts guaranteed or based on live prices?

No. The current forecasts use illustrative fixed market assumptions and randomized paths. You can inspect those assumptions in the simulation panel. Outcomes are educational scenarios, not guarantees or financial advice.

Can I try it before creating an account?

Yes. All three demo profiles let you edit nodes, run simulations, and export scenarios without signing in. Your edits do not overwrite the shared demo profiles.
