Getting started

Do I need an account or a bank connection?

No account is needed for the demo, and no bank connection is required. FinApp uses manual entries, sample profiles, and scenario JSON imports. It does not move real money, place trades, or connect to your bank.

Which demo should I choose?

Mid-Career Family is the default and shows income, bills, savings, investments, and an auto loan together. Recent Grad emphasizes student debt and getting started. FIRE Strategist emphasizes investing and a longer-term savings plan. Each uses the same calculation engine.

How do I switch between light and dark?

Use the sun/moon control in the header. Your explicit choice is stored in this browser and is restored when you reload or reopen a public page. Before a choice is saved, the page follows your device preference. Restricted storage or private browsing may limit how long the preference is retained. The dashboard uses the same saved preference and includes its own sun/moon switch in the header.

Saving and accounts

Where does my work live?

Node Graph scenario drafts, including demo edits, are stored in this browser for the selected profile. JSON export/import lets you back them up and move them to another device. Signed-in budgeting and stock records are stored in the application’s PostgreSQL database. Those records and the Node Graph draft are separate; changing one does not automatically update the other.

Will my account appear on a teammate’s computer?

In the current local development setup, each machine has its own database. New registrations belong to that local database. The development testuser account is seeded independently on each machine; it is not one shared cloud account. Git shares application code, not your private account records.

How is registration verified?

In the local classroom setup, the backend prints the six-digit verification code to the Spring Boot terminal. Enter it on the verification page, or use resend if needed. Codes expire after 15 minutes. This setup does not require a paid email provider.

What happens if I close the tab or clear browser data?

Sign-in is tied to the current tab. Sign out or return to a public page to end the private workspace session. Browser session restore can affect tab state. Graph drafts remain saved independently of sign-in, but clearing site storage can remove them. A JSON export is the portable backup for your scenario.

Numbers and simulations

Why does 0% investing still show a contribution?

The extra-investing slider applies only to money left after bills and planned transfers. A scheduled investment connection can still contribute at 0% extra investing. To model no new investment contributions, disable scheduled investment transfers as well. Existing investments can still gain or lose value.

Does a zero contribution make every line fall?

No. A household can keep accumulating cash even when it makes no new investments. A decline needs a reason, such as losses, inflation, or spending more than income. The model does not force downward results merely because the contribution is zero.

What do P10, P50, and P90 mean?

They summarize the distribution of simulated outcomes at each year. P10 has about 10% of outcomes below it; P50 is the midpoint; P90 has about 90% below it. Results can fall outside that band. The lines are neither guaranteed bounds nor three individual market paths.

Are prices live and forecasts certain?

No. Market history is cached from a free provider. The dashboard reports the source, dates, and fallback assumptions when history is unavailable. Stock charts show historical adjusted prices, rather than live trading quotes. Forecasts are educational possibilities and cannot guarantee investment results.

What is the difference between Monte Carlo and Markov?

Monte Carlo explores many possible return sequences, using historical blocks or manual return assumptions. Markov adds persistent stronger and weaker market states and their transitions. Both apply the same household budget rules. The How it works page explains each model and its limits.

How is income tax calculated?

Gross pay is reduced by the effective percentage you choose, initially 20% for new demo scenarios. This is an estimate, not a calculation of federal or state tax brackets. When income is entered as take-home pay, the model does not subtract tax again.
