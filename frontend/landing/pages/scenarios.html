FINAPP / DEMO SCENARIOS

Three starting points.
Plenty of possibilities.

Explore a different financial situation without creating an account. Change the numbers, compare the outcomes, and make the scenario your own.

Choose a starting point close to the question you want to ask. Each profile uses the same model; the starting balances, monthly bills, and planned transfers differ. The figures below reflect the seeded examples, with gross pay and a 20% effective tax estimate.

PROFILE 01

Recent Grad

Build a foundation while paying down debt.

Start with $1,800 in checking, $1,200 in a Roth IRA, and $30,000 of student loans. The initial loan payments total $450 per month, living expenses are $1,400, and the plan invests $200.

A useful experiment

Try reducing extra investing while increasing your debt-payment plan. Compare the debt strategies, then inspect how a higher expense assumption changes the cash buffer.

PROFILE 02 / DEFAULT DEMO

Mid-Career Family

Balance today’s commitments with tomorrow’s plans.

Start with $6,200 in checking, $88,000 in a 401(k), $15,000 in savings, and a $14,000 auto loan. Living expenses total $4,600, the loan payment is $450, and planned transfers direct $1,100 to investments and $300 to savings.

A useful experiment

Try a lower forward-return assumption or higher living-cost growth. Watch how the loan payoff releases monthly cash, and compare the inflation-adjusted result with nominal dollars.

PROFILE 03

FIRE Strategist

Explore the trade-offs behind a savings-focused plan.

Start with $2,500 in checking, $117,000 in investments, $20,000 in savings, and no outstanding debt. Living expenses are $2,200. Planned percentage transfers initially invest about $3,033.34 and save $216.67 per month.

A useful experiment

Try changing the planned allocation and market assumptions. A long horizon magnifies small differences, but the model does not automatically stop income or begin retirement withdrawals.

READ THE DEMO CORRECTLY

A starting point, ready for your what-if.

The Family profile is the default. Selecting Recent Grad or FIRE opens that specific scenario. The Overview is the first dashboard tab, and the Node Graph gives you the editable financial map.

Changing a demo creates a browser-local scenario draft. It does not overwrite the seeded example for other people. Export your JSON before switching devices or clearing browser data. Reset restores the original server profile.

After-bill surplus is not all disposable spending: planned savings and investment transfers still need to be funded. Incomplete costs make a plan look stronger than it really is, so add realistic expenses before interpreting the forecast.
